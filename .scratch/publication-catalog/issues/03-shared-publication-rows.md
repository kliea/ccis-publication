# Is a shared publication one row or one row per researcher?

Type: grilling
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:
Assignee: wayfinder

## Question

When two or more researchers are authors of the same publication, is that one table row, or one row per researcher?

Invoke grilling and domain-modeling. The answer records the row rule and any term it locks in `CONTEXT.md`.

## Answer

One publication is one row, including when two or more researchers are authors of it. Author/s lists the full byline, including authors who are not on the roster. Title, journal, charge, ranking, ISSN, and DOI appear once. "Continue" accepted this recommended rule.

Terms locked in `CONTEXT.md`: **Row**, **Author/s**.
