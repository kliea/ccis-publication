# Where does a researcher with no 2025 publication appear?

Type: grilling
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:
Assignee: wayfinder

## Question

When a researcher has no publication in 2025, does the site show that researcher with an empty result, or only researchers who have at least one row?

Invoke grilling and domain-modeling.

## Answer

The publication table lists publications only. A researcher with no 2025 publication has no row and does not appear as an empty result. "Continue" accepted this recommended rule. Whether a separate roster page names those researchers is a later ticket.

The **Row** term in `CONTEXT.md` now includes this.
