# Who may start a refresh?

Type: grilling
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 15
Assignee: wayfinder

## Question

A refresh replaces the snapshot when someone asks. Who is allowed to start that refresh?

Invoke grilling and domain-modeling. This waits on what the spec assumes the site is.

## Answer

The user chose whoever rebuilds and publishes the pages. The spec names no role and no account. Holding the files is what allows the refresh.

**Refresh** is locked in `CONTEXT.md`.
