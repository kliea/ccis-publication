## Question

A refresh replaces the snapshot when someone asks. Cherry I. Tabada, Jessie Lynde M. Poralan, Junward B. Megullas, and Fritzie T. Nuez were searched only with the middle initial, and that search found nobody. Does a refresh search those four names again without the middle initial?

Invoke grilling and domain-modeling.

## Answer

"Continue" accepted a second search. The next refresh searches Cherry I. Tabada, Jessie Lynde M. Poralan, Junward B. Megullas, and Fritzie T. Nuez without the middle initial. A clear match is bound by the same rule as everyone else. Nobody found still adds nothing, and that researcher is then unmatched. Later refreshes do not repeat this special search.

Mary Pilar J. Barber and Robert Lee M. Bawiga were already searched both ways. They are not part of this.

The **Unchecked researcher** term is locked in `CONTEXT.md`.
