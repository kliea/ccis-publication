## Question

Column rules, row shape, the snapshot, and the byline mark are decided. What does one screen of the publication table look like, including a marked roster name, an unmarked outside author, and a blank Database, Indexing, Ranking / JIF, and ISBN cell?

Invoke the prototype skill. Link the artifact from this ticket. The choice of which take to keep is the user's.

## Answer

The user chose the ledger, with a modal for the detail. The screen is one wide table. Clicking a row opens that publication's full columns over the table. Close, the backdrop, and Escape dismiss it. A link in the row does not open the modal. Roster names stay bold. The other two takes stay in the throwaway file for comparison.

Artifact: [publication-table.html](../prototype/publication-table.html) (`?variant=A`).
