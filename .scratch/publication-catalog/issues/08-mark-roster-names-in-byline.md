# Are roster researchers marked in the Author/s cell?

Type: grilling
Label: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:
Assignee: wayfinder

## Question

Author/s lists every author on the publication. Does that cell mark which names are researchers on the roster, or are all names shown the same way?

Invoke grilling and domain-modeling.

## Answer

"Continue" accepted the mark. In Author/s, a researcher's name is marked. An author who is not on the roster is shown unmarked. The look of the mark is left to [What does the publication table look like?](./11-publication-table-prototype.md).

The **Author/s** term in `CONTEXT.md` now includes this.
